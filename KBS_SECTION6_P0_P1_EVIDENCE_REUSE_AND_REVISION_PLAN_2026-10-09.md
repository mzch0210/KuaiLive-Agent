## Stage 4 — Whole-section acceptance checklist

1. **Statistical honesty:** Every p-value/CI has a defined estimand, sampling unit and conditionality. Do not treat repeated seeds on same users as independent users or observational state contrasts as causality.
2. **Method originality:** Show what predicting `Δ_M` changes in routing at fixed call budgets versus a prediction of `u(M)`, Base weakness and transparent heuristic; if all are similar, narrow novelty from "unique superiority" to a clearly articulated operational valuation diagnostic.
3. **Cross-platform separation:** Every KuaiLive number is accompanied by the exact protocol and checkpoint; no sampled/full native metric is mixed with the same-checkpoint 6.3 swap.
4. **Candidate validity:** Sampled-active and full-active are distinguishable; official-exposure-negative protocol is not a direct substitute for sampled-active; do not infer unknown unexposed items to be true negatives.
5. **Result density:** Main 6.1–6.5 retains its two principal Twitch results figures/tables and KuaiLive 2×2, with at most one compact new budget plot if the analysis is credible; 20-row component and multiple grid panels live in formal supplements.
6. **No TEST recycling as independent evidence:** Supplementary notes label all pre-inspected TEST analyses accurately. A future independent TEST claim requires truly unused evaluation data or predeclared prospective evaluation.
7. **Build and package:** Resolve all manuscript↔supplement labels, verify LaTeX/PDF layouts and bibliography; separate author signoff. Sections 7–8 and Abstract remain outside this scoped plan.

## Suggested stage gates / no-repeat decision logic

- **Gate A (immediate):** Evidence identity ledger completed; provenance of every reused metric verified. **No GPU run**.
- **Gate B (DEV only):** Convert saved OOF predictions to common-budget curves and import completed Memory component matrix; freeze analysis scripts and keep null/negative findings. Optional DEV gate-target comparison if curve alone does not distinguish methods.
- **Gate C (P2 conditional):** Run only previously unperformed same-checkpoint repeated-sampling P2 and record predetermined seeds/sizes/hash. No new checkpoint training, unless the originally verified ones are absent and regeneration is independently authorized.
- **Gate D (main text):** Amend 5/6.1–6.4 only where necessary to incorporate verified findings. Keep 6.5 intact. Do not use stronger words than the evidence allows.
- **Gate E (submission):** Formal S2/S3/S4/S5/S7/S8/S9, figure/table references and editorial checks, then Section 7.

**External field-level reason to prioritize evaluation controls:** The official [KBS aims/scope](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/knowledge-based-systems/0950-7051) explicitly includes recommender systems and decision support, emphasizing original innovative results and theory/practical balance. Candidate sampling can change model orderings (Krichene and Rendle, *On Sampled Metrics for Item Recommendation*, CACM 2022 / original KDD 2020); published [ACM TOIS evaluation study](https://doi.org/10.1145/3545796) shows model rankings depend on optimization and evaluation settings. These motivate rigorous controls rather than compulsory extra experiments.
