## Why this differs from N11

N11's Introduction repeated background, project-by-project evidence summaries, and 3 contributions that read as three independent projects. Its vertical P1→P2→P3 figure suggested a causal sequence even though P3 is a parallel historical-source validity question. Related Work grouped reasonable papers, but repeated the sentence "not a generic theorem/gate" and a restatement of our difference after nearly every paragraph.

C2 now follows a single research object: the ranking-utility **increment of an unchanged, explicit relationship source over a fixed incumbent under current candidates**.

1. Motivation: persisted viewer–creator evidence is available outside Base's sequence, but may be redundant with it. Ask about incremental *use-time value* rather than specialist accuracy.
2. Prior problem landscape: representation/fusion or memory admission (MHGFCL, SAMD), decision-to-defer (Mozannar/Sontag, Mao, DS-Frame) and candidate-sensitive sampled ranking metrics (Krichene/Rendle) provide tools and precedents.
3. Define the same event-level ranking difference between Base and Memory on eligible candidates. Observed target and retrospective outcomes may generate labels but are never features at serving time.
4. Develop TWO consequence questions: downstream value prediction and specialist choice (RQ2); separately test whether the authentic history supplies information with current candidates and ranker fixed (RQ3). Both depend on the RQ1-defined value object, neither on the other's realized policy outcome.
5. Outline Twitch/LiveRec streamer, original KuaiLive room and M3 persistent author evidence roles without treating them as identical populations; distinguish initial forward-time M3 TEST from 30,000/4,096 newly evaluated target event IDs in the already studied source period.
6. The three contributions are empirically operationalized, not presented as inventions of a new generic routing theorem, a production cost gain or a new sampled-metric inconsistency fact.
