## Source-to-claim controls

- **Twitch frozen TEST (primary C3):** 44,221 test events, DEV-fitted model and DEV-frozen threshold, Utility-vs-Base **+0.00772 NDCG@10** (95% paired CI **[+0.00641,+0.00903]**). The fine-grained comparator details stay in §§5 and 6.2; no TEST model refit or threshold retuning took place.
- **KuaiLive candidate evaluation (C2):** 10,222 examined test events on the same cohort, fixed checkpoint when varying candidate size/membership; three training seeds and nine negative-sampling draws are sensitivity analyses, not 3×9 independent holdouts. Difference under sampled-active/full-active is the protocol-specific **−0.04120 to +0.04631** Memory–Base NDCG shift, a descriptive comparison rather than a causal intervention on candidate count.
- **Retrospective vs observable (C1/C3):** target-defined represented/recoverable/unavailable and recurrence bins are observable only after the target event for diagnosis. All 14 selector descriptors are constructed from prior user history and Base scores; no target-derived feature enters the Twitch frozen predictor.
- **Evaluation granularity:** Both datasets use one observed positive target per evaluated request and event-specific eligible candidates, not exhaustive relevance labels. The Twitch source is a ten-minute viewer/chat snapshot protocol, not precise arrival/departure ground truth. The two platforms have different targets, incumbent models and event/candidate eligibility; their absolute NDCG values are not an interchangeable benchmark.
- **CPU replay provenance:** Supplementary S9 confirms 3 hosted CPU replicas × 1,200 warmed queries, single thread, candidate count 575; *gate prediction timed*, **route decisions replayed from precomputed masks** rather than from the gate's freshly timed output. Prepared candidate feeds/history/cache, network I/O, loading, concurrent workload and state maintenance are omitted. Consequently *controlled warmed scoring overhead* is the measured quantity, not realized deployment end-to-end latency.
- **Realistic cross-platform validity:** No independent, frozen, zero-shot Twitch→KuaiLive utility gate trial is present. Future deployment claims need separately designed and frozen new-regime trials, preferably explicit transfer-versus-retraining protocols and prospective serving experiments.
- **Nearest external published evaluation context:** [Krichene & Rendle, *On sampled metrics for item recommendation*, *Communications of the ACM* 65(7):75–83 (2022), DOI 10.1145/3535335](https://doi.org/10.1145/3535335), already cited in manuscript §7.2, establishes that sampled metric model orderings may change; [*Improving Methodological Standards in Recommender Systems Offline Evaluation*, *ACM Transactions on Recommender Systems* (2026), DOI 10.1145/3800587](https://doi.org/10.1145/3800587) is a post-publication independent reason to maintain offline/outcome separation, not an extra manuscript citation required in §7.4. Source details for the actual manuscript are the frozen experiment and S1–S9, not these background references.
