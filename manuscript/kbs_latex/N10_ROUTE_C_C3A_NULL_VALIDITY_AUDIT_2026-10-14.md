## Important statistical and causal interpretation

1. The three seeds are *three randomized counterfactual histories for the same evaluation requests*. They are not three independent replication datasets and must not be subjected to an independent-samples three-study meta-analysis.
2. Null labels alter viewer–creator repeat frequency and higher-order structure as well as specific pairs. Their contrasts are effects of a composite perturbation, not clean identification of a unique pairwise preference mechanism.
3. Fixing Full-F candidates directly counters the simple claim that the entire original-vs-null gap was *only* due to changing evaluation candidate sets, but it does not eliminate every temporal or exposure-related confound in the observational dataset.
4. The B2 same-DEV popularity floor cannot be borrowed to certify B4-A's predeclared different-cohort floor; likewise post-A4 calculated popularity values could at most be a transparent exploratory sensitivity check, not proof of the original prereveal quality gate.
5. B4-A user and recording period overlap with earlier accessed M3 source. Only source-event ID overlap with prior **evaluated targets** is zero; thus new same-period target-specific frozen analysis, not independent time generalization or unseen user replication.
