## Scientific estimand: what must a corrected-S arm estimate?

One-positive per request NDCG@10 (u_F(A,x)=1/log_2(r_F(A,x)+1)) if (r_F\le 10), else 0. Decision label (Delta_F(x)=u_F(M,x)-u_F(B,x)), conditional target (eta_F(z)=E[Delta_F(X)\mid Z=z]). Sampled S supplies only (u_S(M,x),u_S(B,x)) under target-retained 574-negative without-replacement sampling.

- Krichene & Rendle KDD2020 show sampled ranking metrics can fail to preserve full-candidate comparisons, and proposed bias/MSE corrective mapping for **ranking metric estimation**: https://research.google/pubs/on-sampled-metrics-for-item-recommendation/ .
- Li et al. KDD2020 analyze a sampled/full **hit-rate** metric mapping: https://www.kdd.org/kdd2020/accepted-papers/view/on-sampling-top-k-recommendation-evaluation.html .
- Jin et al. AAAI2021 discuss sampled metric estimators and limitations of different sampled/global metrics: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/16537 .
- None of these sources, merely by existence, establishes a per-event unbiased F-NDCG@10 label or conditional (eta_F(z)) recovered from a single censored sampled NDCG scalar. For any scientifically valid correction, first specify whether it targets (E[u_F]\), conditional (E[u_F\mid Z]\), the exact per-event u_F, or policy allocation regret; do not conflate them.
- A corrected-S label requiring real F outcome labels would be an **F-assisted hybrid**, not a purely sampled-supervised counterfactual. Metrics methods that require pooled corpus popularity/global priors or known full ranks also need to declare that information contract before experiments.
- An estimator that provably improves aggregate ranking-metric MSE may still perform badly on **event-conditioned relative model advantage** and ultimately policy decisions, so DEV-only comparison would need both label-error and downstream switch/quality, with same B/M/Z and nested user folds.
