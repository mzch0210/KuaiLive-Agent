## Overall independent assessment

**Section 7 is scientifically coherent, reviewer-tightened, numerically consistent with its governing evidence, and technically accepted in the exact-source build.** No unresolved P0 claim contradiction or new experiment requirement was found for the existing C1–C3. The operative central thesis is:

> An auxiliary historical-relationship ranker's knowledge value is its **marginal ranking utility relative to a specified trained Base over an eligible candidate regime**; observed context dependence motivates **pre-outcome prediction of that relative value** and selective use, while computational feasibility must be evaluated separately.

This is a *decision-oriented interpretation and empirically validated test* of historical knowledge, rather than a novel subtraction identity, a new universal learning-to-defer algorithm, an intrinsic reliability score, or proof of an online engagement effect. The result is restricted to the fixed Base–specialist arrangements and the reported two live-streaming offline protocols.
