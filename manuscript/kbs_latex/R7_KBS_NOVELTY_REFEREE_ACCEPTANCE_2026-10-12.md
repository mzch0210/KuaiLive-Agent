## 2. Independent referee-style major-objection test

1. **Novelty objection (major; treated):** Relative-utility allocation follows existing learning-to-defer; KBS already publishes internal knowledge gates. The introduction, R7 §2 edits and §7 locate the evidence contribution in a fixed relationship specialist's event-ranking incremental NDCG and the two TEST-frozen decision protocols. This supports a distinguishable experimental question, **not proven algorithmic novelty**. Residual editorial risk: empirical significance and KBS preference for method novelty remain real; do not estimate acceptance probability.
2. **Comparator objection (major; preserved):** Models from CKLF/MHGFCL/KAPER are **not retrained as identical-data baselines**; the paper does **not** establish SOTA superiority over them. All M3 Utility/Difficulty/Specialist gate predictors use the same 12 observables, Uncertainty/History are simpler designated heuristics. Cross-method claims are about these five frozen objectives only. For Twitch, equal-count Difficulty is retrospectively TEST-ranked, unlike M3.
3. **Resource-constraint objection (major; accepted adverse evidence):** M3 unconditional Memory NDCG@10 **0.346792** exceeds all six Utility scores **0.301766–0.304004** and warmed DEV 256-event Memory mean **0.8033 ms** is below the Utility route **1.8018 ms**. Under these measured cost/quality axes the gate is not optimal. A hard external expert-call constraint is **stipulated**, not proven economically necessary. Never advertise end-to-end speedup or automatic production benefit.
4. **Budget comparability objection (major; scope):** M3 five selectors share a maximum 30% DEV optimization cap, **not equal TEST rates**; History TEST **35.4724%** and multiple other policies exceed 30%. Absolute TEST NDCG and realized calls are both disclosed, avoiding an invalid equal-cost superiority claim. A genuinely exact-cost-matched prospective comparison is **not** present; treat as residual interpretation risk, not a fabricated experiment.
5. **Scientific independence objection (major; scope):** Twitch 44,221 held-out streamer decisions versus M3 47,575 held-out author decisions are two distinct frozen settings. Original KuaiLive room 10,222 same-event candidate reversal is retrospective. Six M3 2×3 model fits share the same 12,533 users; inference uses three Holm comparisons **within each fit**, not global familywise control across 18. The six results are robustness, not independent population replications.
6. **Causality and future information objection (major; scope):** M3 decisions use only earlier observed histories, including chronologically prior TEST events, with TEST target labels accessed only after scores and policies freeze. Original crossed candidate allocation includes size, composition and competition in membership; does not identify a pure list-length causal effect.
7. **Visibility and reproducibility objection (R8 BLOCK):** Event-level artifacts/weight rights and retention need legitimate author-approved archiving. A GitHub Actions time-limited artifact ID is not a permanent source. R6 supplementary accurately reports the limitation; do **not** suggest that an external release or DOI exists.
8. **Editorial density objection (R8):** R5 36-page main plus R6 33-page supplement merit compression in final submission if readability/publisher requirements so demand; **no verified journal-specific page cap** is assumed. Full PDF visual and frontmatter QA must be repeated after an Abstract is added.
