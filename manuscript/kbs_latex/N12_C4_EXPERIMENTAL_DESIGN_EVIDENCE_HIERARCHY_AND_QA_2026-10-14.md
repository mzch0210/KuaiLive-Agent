## Scholarly problem addressed

The previous Experimental Setup chapter mixed platform descriptions, Base/comparator definitions, time protocols, full-vs-sampled labels, retrospective nulls, additional same-period targets, randomization tests and latency details into chronological blocks. It was hard to tell which comparison answered which of RQ1–RQ3, and which supplied frozen primary evidence rather than an exploratory mechanism. Twitch equal-TEST-count retrospective controls are not original M3 frozen pointwise policies.
3. **Development Calibration and Pre-Outcome Information.** Covers Twitch five-fold DEV OOF, original M3 five user-disjoint DEV OOF folds and 30% design call cap, checkpoint screening with a separate sampled panel, chronological history updates, deterministic ties, and strict target/outcome exclusion from actual decision inputs. 4. **Candidate-Regime and Historical-Source Controls.** Original within-event sampled-to-full candidate contrast with two exact unaltered factorial formulas; separate exploratory M3 five-outer/four-inner user-disjoint S/F DEV selectors; separate 1,024 DEV real/history-null control with closed-hour activation, source activity margins and independent per-seed rewires on the same cohort.

6. **Endpoints, Statistical Inference, and System Measurements.** Event NDCG@10, HR@10, realized calls; resampling by users with different 5,000/3,000/2,000 draw families where appropriate, original per-fit three-contrast Holm TEST family, and separately precommitted additional-target 3-contrast Holm family with eligible-population twenty-cell weights; exact NDCG and 4,096 nested-null estimands.
