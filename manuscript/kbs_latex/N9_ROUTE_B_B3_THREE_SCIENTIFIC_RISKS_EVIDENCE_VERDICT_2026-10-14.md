## 2. R1 — Originality of knowledge valuation for decision support

**Verdict: MATERIAL IMPROVEMENT; STILL BOUNDED as a journal-level originality claim.**

B1A decomposes E3a mean source×candidate interaction +0.115382: exact-zero-in-either F/S source-gap events contribute **+0.100885** (87.4% of net signed mean), so much of the cross-regime reversal is consistent with **known sampled top-10 support effects**. This is not a novel sampled-metric theorem (Krichene and Rendle KDD 2020).

E1 demonstrates concrete *downstream request allocation*: 21.32% SASRec and 23.01% GRU4Rec of M3 author requests switch decisions under S versus F supervision at similar actual total specialist calls, with F full-ranking gains +0.006159 and +0.004970. Actual calls differ (S/F 14988/15016 and 15001/14762), and full-candidate labels entail greater training-information access. These are descriptive conditional allocation findings, not proof of algorithm-first superiority.

**New source-specific support beyond F-vs-S metric comparison:** Route B B2 evaluates original and time/degree/author-availability-preserving randomized relationship histories under the **same F full candidate roster**, not a changed sampled-metric scale. F real-minus-valid-null Full Memory NDCG means are +0.032858, +0.035544, +0.034027; all user-cluster 95% intervals lie above zero. This provides an additional empirical reason why historical *identity arrangement* can matter to knowledge valuation. However, 78.6% strong rewiring also changes repeat-pair continuity and higher-order relations, so the result does **not** isolate one semantic causal variable or prove domain-universal novelty.

**Admissible scientific contribution:** An empirical incumbent-relative knowledge-value and candidate-aware decision analysis whose knowledge-specific stakes extend beyond sampled evaluation and whose downstream request allocation is observable. No new selector theorem or cost superiority claim.

## 3. R2 — Decision validity and invocation constraints

**Verdict: BOUNDED; deployment claim still unsupported.**

E2's whole observed quality–realized-call envelope gives optimality for the 30%-inner-cap policy under assumed NDCG-equivalent per-call shadow costs of [0.084516,0.111321] (SASRec) and [0.083119,0.104379] (GRU4Rec). These intervals are **idealized point estimates with zero gate overhead**, not measured prices, serving latency or externally justified scarcity. On frozen M3 TEST and warmed DEV scoring, Always Memory remains a strong counterexample to unconditional selective routing efficiency. Different information entitlements and 28/239 net call discrepancies preclude a new strict equal-budget algorithm claim. B2 does not cure R2.

**Admissible contribution:** valuation-informed allocation under a stipulated knowledge-invocation constraint, with transparent limits.

## 4. R3 — Integrated significance and relation-identity mechanism

**Verdict: STRONG WITHIN THE SPECIFIC EXPLORATORY DEV CONTROL DESIGN; BOUNDED OUTSIDE THAT DESIGN.** This is not "three risks all solved".

Route B B2, primary **fixed Full Memory under unchanged full F candidate roster**, compared original historical author identity to three **historically time-valid, author-degree/activity-preserving** nulls on 1,024 requests (992 users):

| Null seed | Original F NDCG@10 | Null F NDCG@10 | Real-minus-null F | User-cluster 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 20261014 | 0.339348 | 0.306491 | +0.032858 | [0.023317, 0.042538] |
| 20261015 | 0.339348 | 0.303804 | +0.035544 | [0.026723, 0.045122] |
| 20261016 | 0.339348 | 0.305321 | +0.034027 | [0.023890, 0.044384] |

Three-seed descriptive mean +0.034143, range +0.032858…+0.035544. **Do not** treat these random null seeds as three independent datasets or compute an unjustified CI by pooling them. Each per-seed user-bootstrap interval has half-width below the **pre-frozen 0.015 precision threshold**: no 2,048-event expansion is required or permitted for significance hunting.

All nulls passed their frozen non-floor diagnostic; F null NDCG 0.303804–0.306491, far above popularity-only 0.021867, with 476–487 nonzero F top-10 target cases. Original Full S 0.663896 also exceeds null S 0.618221–0.623869, indicating an effect under both retrospective regimes but with somewhat larger S contrasts. This is a **different estimand** from Full-minus-Long source×candidate interaction, which was itself 87.4% net-credited to zero-support transitions.

**Competing explanations remaining:** A strong randomized edge intervention can disrupt genuine repeated pair patterns, subtle co-visitation clusters and higher-order relations despite fixed hourly marginals; arbitrary selection of one null distribution is not universal identification of relationship semantics; one DEV data period does not establish temporal external transfer; no independent confirmatory TEST of B2. This is evidence for sensitivity to correctly arranged historical interaction identities **within this precise perturbation**, not a globally identified causal source mechanism.
