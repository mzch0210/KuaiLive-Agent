## 4. Critical non-floor and intervention strength checks

All three nulls passed the predeclared non-floor conditions. Null Full F NDCG is 0.303804–0.306491 versus popularity-only **0.021867** and original Full F **0.339348**; between 476 and 487 of the 1,024 null-scored requests still place the target in the top ten. Control Full S remains 0.618221–0.623869 (vs predeclared 0.20 floor). Each request is exposed to causal changed history.

However, the present null changes **78.6%** of eligible selected-user relation edges, much more than the old 128-event prefix pilot's **23.2%**. The intervention preserves degree and availability marginals but necessarily disrupts repeated viewer–creator pair identity, temporal pair continuity, and other higher-order relational structure. Therefore the difference quantifies sensitivity to a *strong, degree/time/availability-preserving relation-label randomization*, not the isolated average treatment effect of a uniquely defined friendship mechanism. Do not call the different 128-event pilot result a failed replication; populations and intervention strengths differ.
