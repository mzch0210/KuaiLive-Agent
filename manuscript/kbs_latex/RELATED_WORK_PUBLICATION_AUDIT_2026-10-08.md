## High-priority published-version decisions

1. **KuaiLive:** the canonical paper is [SIGIR 2026, pp.3356–3364](https://researchr.org/publication/sigir-2026), DOI [10.1145/3805712.3808587](https://doi.org/10.1145/3805712.3808587), with ten authors. The [official KuaiLive authors' citation](https://imgkkk574.github.io/KuaiLive/) and [DBLP formal conference index](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/sigir/QuDGZZWNHLX26) corroborate the proceedings identity. The 2025 seven-author arXiv record is an older draft and **is not cited in the manuscript**. ACM direct article text could not be fetched through the verification browser, although publisher DOI and independent formal indices corroborate the record.
2. **AISTATS 2026:** Online Learning-to-Defer with Varying Experts appears as a [published PMLR 300 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/montreuil26b.html) (pp.2701–2709), not as an arXiv-only item. Mao et al. is likewise the [official ICML 2025 PMLR 267 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/mao25c.html). The ICML 2020 and NeurIPS 2022 foundational references point to their own final proceedings.
3. **INFORMS year anomaly:** [Abbas & Hazen, Decision Analysis](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/deca.2024.0187) says 'Cite as (2024)' and records online release 27 Aug 2024, even though volume 22(1) is assigned to March 2025. BibTeX `year={2024}` now follows the publisher's prescribed citation; existing internal key `abbas2025voi` remains unchanged to preserve LaTeX references. A note explains the 2025 issue assignment.
4. **DSS 2024 chronology:** Chen & Liu, *Modeling interaction behavior and preference decline...*, is the [April 2024 final issue article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dss.2023.114146) in volume 179, though its DOI includes 2023. We retain the 2024 publication year.
5. **Most recent formal KBS papers:** the cited 2026 papers have identifiable final volumes/articles including [MCD4SR (346:116174)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116174), [KAPER (347:116236)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116236), and [reliability-aware multimodal recommendation (348:116352)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116352). The uncited CollaPPA paper also has a formal [347:116234](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.116234) version. Publication recency alone does not warrant appending every 2026 item to the narrative.

## Method-description validity and limitations

- The published abstracts/highlights support the Related Work claims about multimodal fusion, denoising, reliability, graph-path evidence, sequential representations, online deferral and streaming recommendation. Formal publishers or proceedings were used for those comparisons.
- `yerragunta2026gating` studies question answering with incomplete knowledge bases, not recommender performance. It remains explicitly a cross-domain gating analogy.
- KAPER includes graph paths in **LLM-oriented re-ranking/explanation**; it is not evidence that its mechanism implements a Base-relative specialist router. Current text correctly frames it as evidence selection.
- Publisher abstracts and metadata were checked; paywalled full-paper experimental details were not independently reimplemented or verified. Stronger claims than those in published abstracts require full-text inspection.
