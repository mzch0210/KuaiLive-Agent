## 1. Objective and governing principles

### 1.1 Objective

Maximize the probability that a future KBS submission is scientifically credible by resolving the objections most likely to determine the editorial decision:

1. What is the incremental contribution beyond sampled-ranking evaluation, ranking correction and learning-to-defer?
2. Does the observed F-supervision advantage persist under a defensible common-resource comparison?
3. Is the original uncorrected sampled-supervision comparator too weak, and can a valid sampled-only corrected competitor be constructed?
4. Does the result generalize to a genuinely unseen cohort or time period?
5. Are knowledge-source and feature-panel claims supported by the available evidence?
6. Is there a credible resource-use motivation, given the adverse Always-Memory and runtime evidence?

### 1.2 Principles

- **No duplication:** every proposed action must be compared with the complete experiment/revision history and the N15 no-repeat contract.
- **No retrospective confirmation:** reused DEV results remain exploratory; revealed TEST data must not be reused for fitting, tuning, hypothesis selection or new confirmatory claims.
- **No causal overstatement:** the original N14 F/S comparison is a complete-policy contrast, not an isolated label-only causal intervention.
- **No false deployment claim:** offline exact-quota batch allocation is not an online policy and does not establish latency, serving feasibility or cost savings.
- **No universal NO-GO claims:** a failed method candidate or dataset route does not prove that all methods or datasets are impossible.
- **No silent rights expansion:** the user's narrow authorization for the internal P1-D diagnostic does not establish public redistribution rights, blanket data clearance, or permission for future analyses.
- **No manuscript-first response:** new results enter the manuscript only after their estimand, evidence tier, scientific value and author approval are adjudicated.
- **Human ownership:** authorship, CRediT, funding, conflicts, ethics, AI-use disclosure, data rights and final submission decisions require responsible-author confirmation.

## 4. Paper-level contribution and claim boundary

### 4.1 Keep one central research question

> When the incremental value of a fixed historical relationship Memory depends on the active candidate regime, does aligning the supervision regime used to train a selective knowledge-use policy with the full active candidate regime change frozen policy decisions and full-candidate ranking quality?

This is a bounded empirical question about **candidate-regime-indexed supervision of incumbent-relative knowledge value**. It is not a claim to have invented sampled ranking correction, a generic learning-to-defer algorithm, a new ranking metric or a universal knowledge router.

### 4.2 Provisional contribution statement

> This study examines a specific decision-design problem in live-streaming recommendation: the candidate universe used to label the incremental value of a fixed historical relationship Memory may differ from the candidate universe under which that Memory is ultimately used. In a controlled, fixed-Base/fixed-Memory setting, the study compares complete selective-policy configurations trained with full-active versus original uncorrected target-retained sampled supervision and evaluates their outcomes on common full-active candidates. The evidence supports a bounded empirical policy difference in the tested setting; it does not establish a label-only causal effect, universal superiority over valid corrected sampled supervision, online cost savings or generalization beyond the observed cohorts.

This statement is provisional. It may be strengthened only if new, valid evidence supports a more specific incremental contribution.

### 4.3 Claim hierarchy

| Claim | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Candidate-regime supervision can change downstream full-candidate policy outcomes in the tested setting | Retain as the central bounded empirical claim, with complete-policy and cohort qualifications. |
| Exact-call offline batch F policy outperforms original S on reused DEV | Retain only as exploratory N15 evidence, not as independent confirmation. |
| Full versus Long-only interaction is a stable knowledge-source mechanism | Do not claim; observed feature-panel dependence makes this unsupported. |
| F supervision is superior to every corrected-S alternative | Do not claim; corrected-S remains an open competitor. |
| F policy is more efficient, lower latency or cheaper than Always Memory | Do not claim; end-to-end F-selector cost is unmeasured and old timing evidence is adverse. |
| The result generalizes to new users, future periods or other domains | Do not claim without a genuinely eligible independent cohort. |
| The work proposes a new general deferral method, rank correction, metric or theorem | Do not claim on current evidence. |

### Phase 2 — Corrected-S competitor eligibility review (method-only; no real policy training)

**Purpose:** address the most important unresolved alternative without repeating NI-1.

**Starting point:** NI-1g is final for the candidates it evaluated. Do not rerun rank expansion, uniform-prior posterior, the same EM/LP candidates, V2a or V2b. Do not reinterpret illustrative synthetic sensitivity parameters as calibrated real-data confidence bounds.

A new candidate may be considered only if it is materially different from the stopped candidates and is supported by a defensible statistical argument. Before any real M3 data use, it must satisfy all of the following:

1. **Information contract:** uses only information genuinely available under the sampled-only training regime; no hidden full labels, full ranks, TEST outcomes or priors estimated from the target full labels.
2. **Estimand:** targets the conditional Memory-minus-Base utility or a clearly defined identified bound relevant to the policy decision—not merely a marginal sampled rank.
3. **Identification:** states assumptions sufficient for identification or partial identification, explains why they are defensible, and identifies observationally equivalent cases where the target is not identified.
4. **Dependence:** models the shared-negative/candidate dependence between Base and Memory rather than assuming independent errors without justification.
5. **Finite-sample and misspecification behavior:** assesses uncertainty, support failure, model misspecification and decision regret; synthetic point-estimation accuracy alone is insufficient.
6. **Prior-art delta:** demonstrates a specific difference from published sampled-rank corrections and deferral methods.
7. **Fair comparison contract:** if admitted later, fixes features, model capacity, folds, calibration, call budget and outcome candidates before any evaluation.

**Method-only sequence:**
- First write a candidate proposal and mathematical estimand.
- Then conduct a source-grounded literature and identifiability review.
- Only if the proposal survives, run public synthetic falsification tests with explicit positive controls and adversarial misspecification.
- No real DEV/TEST training or evaluation is permitted at this stage.

**Deliverable:** `N16_P2_CORRECTED_S_METHOD_ELIGIBILITY_DECISION_*.md`.

**GO:** a genuinely new candidate satisfies the information, identification and prior-art gates. This permits a separately reviewed implementation plan; it does not itself authorize private execution.  
**NO-GO:** no defensible new candidate exists. Close the route for this paper version, preserve NI-1g, and narrow the paper to the original uncorrected-S comparator. Never state that all corrected-S methods are impossible.

### Phase 3 — Independent-cohort data feasibility gate (desk-based; no TEST unsealing)

**Purpose:** determine whether a truly independent confirmation study is possible before designing one.

A candidate dataset/cohort must satisfy all core conditions:

- genuinely unseen target events, not G5/B4-A revealed events or the heavily reused N15 DEV cohort;
- a distinct future recording period for a temporal-generalization claim, or a genuinely disjoint user cohort for a user-generalization claim; state clearly which form of independence is achieved;
- recoverable event-time full-active candidate universe and reproducible candidate construction;
- target timestamps and pre-target history/relationship availability sufficient to reconstruct the original estimand;
- the same fixed Base and Memory comparison can be defined without changing the research question into cold-start or a different task;
- no outcome-informed feature, cohort, threshold or hypothesis selection;
- verified lawful access for internal analysis, and separately verified permissions for publication and public redistribution of data/derivatives;
- adequate eligible event and user coverage for the predeclared estimand and cluster uncertainty.

**Important distinctions:**
- A new time period with some returning users supports temporal validation but is not user-disjoint replication.
- A user-disjoint cohort with no usable historical relationships may change the task into cold-start and is not automatically admissible.
- A public dataset is not automatically suitable; candidate universe, timestamps, history and Memory reconstruction must be demonstrated.
- The failed KuaiRec Big→Small Rev3.1 route remains closed unless genuinely new eligible data and a new protocol meet the conditions above.

**Deliverable:** `N16_P3_INDEPENDENT_COHORT_FEASIBILITY_GATE_*.md`, with source inventory, cohort overlap/time audit, reconstructability, rights, coverage and GO/NO-GO.

**GO:** at least one eligible cohort supports the original target estimand and can be locked before outcomes are inspected.  
**NO-GO:** no eligible cohort exists. Do not manufacture an independent replication from old DEV/TEST. Keep the external-validity limitation explicit and reassess KBS fit.

### Phase 4 — Prospective confirmatory study (conditional; only after Phases 2 and 3 pass)

**No experiment is authorized by this plan.** A separate protocol, data-owner authorization, source/rights gate, code review and responsible-author approval are required.

If admitted, the prospective study should:

1. Freeze the new cohort, eligibility, candidate universe, target events, Base/Memory versions, features, folds, estimands, tie-breaking and analysis code before outcome access.
2. Compare F supervision, original uncorrected target-retained S supervision, and a corrected-S arm **only if Phase 2 admits a valid method**.
3. Use common full-active candidate outcomes and identical, predeclared offline batch call quotas for the primary resource-matched estimand. The primary quota should be fixed before evaluation (q=.30 is a reasonable candidate because it aligns with the existing P1 design, but must be justified prospectively rather than chosen after observing new results).
4. Distinguish exact-quota offline batch allocation from a causal-time threshold policy. Do not describe a batch top-k policy as online deployable.
5. Use the same Base models, Memory definition, feature contract, fitting/calibration protocol and user-disjoint training/validation rules across arms.
6. Define one primary endpoint and one primary contrast before outcomes. A candidate primary estimand is the paired, user-weighted full-candidate NDCG@10 difference at the fixed call quota. Any multi-quota curve, source interaction or secondary ranking metric must be explicitly secondary with multiplicity handling.
7. Use paired user-cluster uncertainty; account for model/fitting uncertainty where feasible and state what remains conditional on fitted policies.
8. Report actual call counts/rates, full Base and Always-Memory endpoints, policy quality, and any adverse results.
9. Keep the new confirmatory hypothesis family separate from the original N14 three-member Holm family. Never revise the original family after seeing its results.
10. Publish only aggregate outputs allowed by the data rights; no raw event/user identifiers, private predictions, private model weights or restricted artifacts.

**Success interpretation:**
- F exceeds S and a valid corrected-S (if included), on the predeclared primary estimand and independent cohort: strongest support for the bounded candidate-regime supervision contribution.
- F exceeds original S but not corrected-S: narrow the contribution to improvement over the original uncorrected sampled supervisor; do not claim superiority over corrected sampling.
- Effects are inconsistent across Base models or cohorts: narrow the claim to the demonstrated conditions.
- The primary effect is absent: report it; reassess the paper's KBS contribution rather than searching further cohorts or feature panels for a positive result.

**Deliverable:** frozen preregistration/protocol, code/source hashes, one-shot result report, independent-cohort provenance and statistical audit.

### Phase 5 — Conditional mechanism, baseline, metric and system-cost work

These are **not default tasks**. They are admitted only when they address a named claim and do not repeat existing work.

- **Feature/source mechanism:** no further same-DEV search. If source-specificity remains essential and a new independent cohort exists, preregister one formal panel-by-source interaction or a single justified mechanism test. Separate interaction inference is required; comparing whether two separate confidence intervals cross zero is not a formal interaction test.
- **Additional selector baselines:** add only if they test a specific alternative explanation and are feasible under the same information and budget contract. Generic additional gates do not automatically create novelty.
- **Additional ranking metrics:** NDCG@5/@20, MRR or HitRate may be secondary robustness outcomes. Do not select a favorable metric after observing the primary result.
- **Runtime/cost:** only measure the vectorized F-selector end-to-end if an independently justified serving/resource constraint exists. Include Memory retrieval, feature extraction, gate inference, batching, hardware and concurrency. Invocation count alone is not cost. Do not infer production savings from the old pointwise timing experiment.
- **Open artifacts:** prepare code, environment, aggregate data and provenance documentation only to the extent that the source-owner and publication rights permit. Reproducibility improves credibility but cannot replace a contribution.

A green GitHub Actions run or successful PDF build is necessary technical evidence, not a scientific novelty verdict.
