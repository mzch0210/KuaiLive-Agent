## R1 — Decision-support scientific distinction: BOUNDED, significantly strengthened

- Exact B1 original nested DEV parity reproduced on the same 50,049 M3 author events / 12,628 users; no G5 TEST.
- The **same realized ~30% knowledge-call fraction hides different selected requests**: SASRec S/F decisions disagree on 10,672 events (**21.32%**); GRU4Rec disagree on 11,517 (**23.01%**).
- For SASRec, F-aligned choice activates Memory for 5,350 events at mean realized F relative utility **0.133417** and excludes it for 5,322 at **0.076194**; contributions +0.014262 and −0.008102 averaged over *all* 50,049 requests; net F NDCG difference **+0.006159** [0.004835, 0.007543]. For GRU4Rec, corresponding switched-on 5,639 at 0.126600, switched-off 5,878 at 0.079135; net **+0.004970** [0.003550, 0.006369].
- This is a concrete conditional knowledge-allocation phenomenon rather than merely juxtaposed sampled ranking metrics. Nevertheless classical sampled metric distortions are already known, and a valid Corrected-S estimator is **not** yet justified by scalar top-10 labels and source artifacts. No priority claim to expert defer regression or rank-sampling theory.

**Admissible main claim:** Candidate-aligned incremental-knowledge supervision leads to different held-out constrained knowledge-use decisions under the same F author-ranking events, with measured decision quality implications.
**Unjustified claim:** A novel universal routing algorithm or a new general sampled-metric correction theorem.

## R2 — Scientific decision utility under a transparent hypothetical constraint: BOUNDED

Using all frozen A1 DEV policies and both fixed endpoints (actual outer calls), the idealized `J_lambda=F_NDCG10-lambda*q`, with zero gate overhead, selects the 30%-inner-limit rule **only when**:
- SASRec shadow cost λ in **[0.084516, 0.111321]** expected NDCG per additional Memory call;
- GRU4Rec λ in **[0.083119, 0.104379]**.

Under λ=0, Always Memory remains the best-accuracy policy; in original M3 warmed offline replay it is also faster than the full selective path. The lambda units are **not** milliseconds or real costs, and the upper-envelope switching thresholds currently lack user-paired uncertainty intervals due to absent joint A1 policy outcomes in the exported aggregate.

**Admissible main claim:** Specialist-allocation decisions have an explicit resource-shadow-price dependence under clearly declared assumptions.
**Unjustified claim:** Observed lower production latency, deployed saving or justified universal 30% resource cap.

## R3 — Integrated relationship-source × candidate × decision evidence: BOUNDED, strengthened with independent estimands but NOT independent data

- B2 fixed-source F/S descriptive reversal was newly **paired and user-cluster bootstrapped** on all 50,049 events. Full-minus-Long under F: **+0.010278** [0.007906, 0.012668]; under retrospective S: **−0.105104** [−0.108326, −0.101683]; raw source×candidate interaction **+0.115382** [0.111724, 0.119065]. This is *not* nested selector-regret interaction.
- Pre-outcome distinct-author-history quartiles have source interactions roughly 0.0483/0.1065/0.1300/0.1768; descriptive subgroup pattern, not separately randomized history mechanism.
- **Zero-mass diagnostic:** 46,462 of 50,049 events have zero Full-minus-Long gap in at least one regime; only **66** have strictly opposite nonzero F/S source-gap signs. Thus the aggregate reversal must not be described as pervasive individual sign flipping.
- A new genuinely historical, **one-hour author-degree-/user-time-preserving co-active relation-null** passed the 128-event DEV pilot. It altered 127,238 of 547,992 available historical interactions in the pretarget prefix, covering 126 users, while preserving the per-hour author margins, user's own timestamps/count and cross-swapped author room eligibility. Original Full/Long F/S values were reproduced exactly before alteration, and the null was **non-floor**. In these last-quarter pilot events, Full F 0.396585 original versus 0.384386 null; sampled S 0.661360 versus 0.669835 null. There is no pilot confidence interval and no population generalization.
- A whole-cohort historically time-respecting identity control would need to apply swaps only after the hour has fully closed for each ranked request, or a pre-all-DEV prefix, rather than allowing same-hour *future* interactions to modify past requests. Current pilot does not establish that complete prospective causal protocol. **Full E3b not executed**; correct identity specificity remains unproved.

**Admissible main claim:** Historical source composition interacts with candidate environment, and aligned utility supervision changes restricted knowledge-use decisions.
**Unjustified claim:** Relationship identity alone is causally proved to determine these interactions, or B1/B2/E1/E3a are independent held-out experiments.

## Joint mechanism contribution and literature boundary

The journal-level contribution is an **empirical decision-support knowledge assessment** with a coherent chain: incumbent-relative utility conditionality → candidate-context source valuation → supervision-induced specific request-choice changes → explicit cost/use boundary. Known prior art includes sampled-metric non-preservation (Krichene & Rendle KDD 2020), expert deferral regression (Mao, Mohri & Zhong ICML 2024), and time-respecting network-null principles (Phys. Rev. E 2018, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.97.052311). None are claimed as new inventions.

It meets the topical KBS remit of original decision-support/knowledge engineering research when scoped as an empirically grounded knowledge-use finding, but the editor may still judge the external novelty and breadth insufficient. A completed and valid full history identity control or truly unseen lawful chronological confirmation would materially strengthen R3, but neither should be fabricated by repeated same-cohort DEV reuse.
