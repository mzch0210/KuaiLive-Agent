> In a post-hoc, user-disjoint development analysis of the same KuaiLive-M3 author-ranking events, matching relative-utility supervision to the full-active candidate regime improved full-active policy NDCG@10 relative to supervision derived from target-retained sampled evaluation. > In matched exploratory source ablations, the ordering of the fixed full Short–Long–Popularity specialist and its Long-only component reversed between full-active and target-retained sampled evaluation, and both relationship-based specialists substantially outperformed popularity-only and history-score placement controls on full-active ranking accuracy.

**Interpretation sentence:**
> These analyses distinguish a candidate-aligned *knowledge valuation problem* from generic expert deferral and show how the choice of ranking regime and historical evidence source shape the utility labels available for learned decisions.

> These findings concern a specified specialist-invocation constraint and do not establish an absolute serving-cost advantage; unconditional Memory retains greater M3 ranking accuracy and lower warmed development-replay scoring time.
