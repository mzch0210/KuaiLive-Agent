## 2. Central claim and evidence contract

**Single thesis:** In the studied offline live-streaming settings, the candidate regime used to supervise the incumbent-relative value of a fixed historical relationship source affects which requests receive that source and the full-candidate quality of the resulting frozen ranking policies.

- **RQ1 (value diagnosis):** the measured increment from Memory depends on the incumbent, usable history and actual candidates. Twitch history-state diagnostics and paired KuaiLive room rankings motivate the target; neither is an independent causal estimate of the M3 author-ranking policy result.
- **RQ2 (primary decision consequence):** separately fitted, DEV-calibrated policies use sampled-active or full-active relative-value labels; the primary 30,000-target comparison reports weighted full-candidate NDCG and naturally realized, population-weighted Memory-call fractions. The sampled comparator is the original uncorrected target-retained protocol. Calls differ by 1.337 and 1.797 percentage points, so this is not a strict matched-call or isolated label-only causal estimate.
- **RQ2 supporting allocation trace:** 50,049 shared DEV events; 21.32% SASRec and 23.01% GRU4Rec decisions switch. This describes fitted allocation and does not causally decompose the separate additional-target result.
- **RQ3 (parallel source-validity check):** fixed full candidates and Memory scorer, authentic history versus three constrained history nulls on a nested 4,096-target cohort. This is a composite source perturbation, not an RQ2 mediator or isolated semantic-causality proof. The same-cohort pre-reveal TRAIN-only Popularity-margin acceptance check remains **NOT VERIFIED**.
- **Operating boundary:** Always Memory scores higher than the selective F-aligned policies on the 30,000-target cohort; the measured legacy pointwise selector is slower than unconditional Memory. No end-to-end deployment or monetary savings claim.
