## C. Novelty distinctions vs identified public prior art

- Krichene & Rendle (KDD 2020) already show sampled rank-metric inconsistency and propose bias/MSE corrections: https://research.google/pubs/on-sampled-metrics-for-item-recommendation/. Therefore sampled S vs F reversal alone **not new**.
- Mao et al. (ICML 2024) already develop learned conditional deferral with pretrained predictor and expert(s): https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/mao24d.html. Base-relative advantage plus routing is not itself new.
- Qian et al. (2026-07-27 Active RAG preprint) already separate marginal retrieval utility, fixed budget, threshold transfer and actual gate overhead: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24010. Knowledge-call utility and budget alone do not suffice for firstness.
- **Defensible empirical intersection:** candidate regime chosen for offline training labels changes *request-level allocations of a fixed historical knowledge source* and the separately frozen full-F ranking outcomes. None of the cited general results, by itself, demonstrates this specific M3 protocol and outcome. However this is an application-specific empirical contribution and may still be viewed as an expected consequence of metric mismatch combined with DFL/deferral; novelty **OPEN**.

### Three real residual science gaps (not covered by existing controls)

1. **Statistically corrected-S instead of original uncorrected target-retained S**: no verified sampled-only corrected estimator has been run; B1 adapted uses F labels. But prior KDD correction targets sampled ranking *evaluation*, not necessarily event-level conditional Δ_F(Z); before any pilot test validity, necessary sampled ranks/probabilities, dev source reconstruction and whether it would distinguish decisions. **V2a remains INFORMATION_AUDIT / no experiment by default**.
2. **Knowledge-specific causal mechanism**: B2 shows sources are informative, while B1A heavily zero-support dependent and H2 historical swaps are composite. It does not isolate relation semantics or prove F/S effect uniquely historical. No new experiment unless seeking that stronger claim.
3. **Strict equal-call and real production deployment**: H1 configuration dominance stands, but new gate serving overhead not measured; one may publish a conditional bounded result without declaring cost savings or pure equal-call effect.
