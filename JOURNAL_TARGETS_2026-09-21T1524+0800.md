# 1. Recommended submission order

## A1 — Expert Systems with Applications (ESWA)

**Overall recommendation: first choice under the user’s combined constraints.**

### Why it fits

- Direct topical precedent: ESWA published **“Live streaming recommendation based on multiple types of repeated behaviors”** in 2025.
- Broad applied-AI scope fits a paper combining recommendation mechanism, dynamic live-streaming application, robustness, and deployment latency.
- Very large publication volume.
- Fast editorial triage and relatively favorable submission-to-acceptance time for a high-volume Q1 applied-AI journal.
- The current paper has substantial application substance rather than only an abstract routing method.

### Current public signals

- 2025 upgraded CAS: **Computer Science 1st tier**; AI / EE / OR subcategories commonly reported as 2nd tier.
- JCR: Q1.
- Current public volume signal: roughly **4,000+ articles/year** in 2025 according to OpenAlex-derived journal profiles.
- Publisher-derived current handling signals reported by journal-profile sources: roughly **5–7 days to first decision**, with submission-to-acceptance around **5–6 months** depending the current reporting window.

### Main risk

Fast desk screening means the manuscript must immediately show:

- a real live-streaming recommendation problem;
- why dynamic availability and repeated streamer relationships matter;
- why always-on memory fails;
- why the conditional-utility framing is useful;
- practical latency/cost evidence.

### Best framing for ESWA

> **Conditional specialist utility for explicit memory in dynamic live-stream recommendation, validated through identity control, temporal regime analysis, adaptive-computation collision defense, and inference-cost decomposition.**

### Sources

- Journal / current metrics: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/expert-systems-with-applications
- 2025 CAS mirror: https://www.ablesci.com/journal/detail?id=pB2jEp
- Topical precedent: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2025.128217

### Current public signals

### Sources

## A3 — Information Processing & Management (IPM)

**Overall recommendation: third choice; strong scientific fit and fast handling, but lower annual publication volume than ESWA/KBS.**

### Why it fits

- Strong alignment with user-information processing, recommendation, confidence, temporal information value, and behavioral heterogeneity.
- The central question “when does long-term information add value beyond current sequential state?” fits an information-processing narrative well.
- Very favorable publisher handling statistics.

### Current public signals

- 2025 upgraded CAS: **Computer Science 1st tier**, Information Systems 1st tier.
- JCR: Q1.
- Time to first decision: about **5 days**.
- Review time: about **96 days**.
- Submission to acceptance: about **147 days**.
- Annual volume is moderate rather than massive: current databases report several hundred articles/year.

### Main risk

A purely algorithmic recommender paper can look narrower than the journal’s information-science identity. The manuscript should emphasize information value, confidence, temporal non-stationarity, and behavioral interpretation.

### Best framing for IPM

> **Heterogeneous value of long-term user information under dynamic recommendation regimes, and selective exploitation of explicit relationship memory based on predicted relative utility.**

### Sources

- Journal metrics: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/information-processing-and-management
- CAS / volume: https://www.ablesci.com/journal/detail?id=r8LYJr

## A4 — Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence (EAAI)

**Overall recommendation: high-throughput alternative, but scientific-scope fit is weaker than ESWA/KBS/IPM unless the manuscript is explicitly framed as an intelligent real-time system application.**

### Why it remains attractive

- Extremely high article volume.
- Strong current impact / Q1 status.
- Good current handling speed.
- The paper contains a real-time intelligent decision component and systems latency evaluation.

### Current public signals

- 2025 upgraded CAS: **Computer Science 1st tier**; Engineering Multidisciplinary 1st tier; AI / Control / EE mostly 2nd tier.
- JCR: Q1.
- 2025 volume reported by current journal databases: roughly **3,300 articles**.
- Current publisher page reports approximately **86 days review time**, **148 days submission to acceptance**, and **17 days acceptance to publication**; another current publisher-mirroring profile reports ~207 days submission to acceptance, indicating that these operational metrics vary by update window.

### Main risk

“Live-stream recommendation” is not naturally an engineering-automation domain. A submission that lacks an engineering-systems story could desk-reject despite the journal’s high throughput.

### Best framing for EAAI

> **A real-time intelligent recommendation system that conditionally activates explicit memory based on predicted incremental utility and deployment cost.**

### Sources

- Journal: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/engineering-applications-of-artificial-intelligence
- 2025 CAS / volume: https://www.ablesci.com/journal/detail?id=pP3GOr

# 6. Final recommendation

The current paper should not be submitted as a generic “memory-augmented recommender.” For journal positioning, present it as:

> **a mechanism-driven study of when explicit relationship memory provides positive incremental predictive utility beyond strong sequential recommendation, how that utility changes across temporal regimes, and how selective use trades off accuracy, invocation, and latency.**

This positioning best supports ESWA / KBS / IPM and minimizes collision with 2026 memory-agent and adaptive-computation work.
